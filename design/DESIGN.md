## Brand & Style
This design system embodies modest couture, feminine poise, and contemporary luxury. Built for young, discerning women seeking elevated modest fashion, the UI evokes warmth, delicate confidence, and high-fashion tactility. 

The aesthetic is a union of **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** and **Soft Tactile Chic**:
- Airy, generous whitespace paired with warm alabaster backgrounds instead of stark clinical white.
- Subtle romanticism channeled through powdery blush gradients, warm chocolate-toned contrasts, and delicate metallic accents.
- Seamless transitions with velvet-like micro-interactions that mirror the draping of fluid chiffon, silk, and modal fabrics.

## Colors
The palette draws direct inspiration from botanical rose tones, fine silk draping, and warm espresso undertones:

- **Primary (`#4A2E2B`)**: Deep Moka Brown. Grounding, luxurious, and softer than pure black. Used for high-contrast typography, primary call-to-actions, and structural borders.
- **Secondary (`#DDA5A5`)**: Dusty Rose. A gentle, romantic mid-tone for decorative highlights, badges, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary (`#C98A7D`)**: Warm Rose Gold. Brings metallic, radiant accents to star ratings, special promotions, and celebratory banners.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FDF6F3`)**: Warm Rose Alabaster. The foundational background canvas providing a comforting, cashmere-like atmosphere.
- **Neutral Muted (`#8C706B`)**: Soft Cocoa. Reserved for secondary text, placeholder states, and metadata labels.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure Silk White for cards and interactive inputs to gently pop above the alabaster base.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm balances high-fashion editorial presence with digital checkout efficiency:

- **Playfair Display**: Brings haute couture heritage, grace, and curved beauty to hero banners, collection headers, and product titles.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: A clean, contemporary sans-serif with subtle warm geometry. Delivers optimal legibility across product details, sizing guides, filters, and prices.
- **Letter Spacing**: Extended letter spacing is intentionally applied to category uppercase tags (`label-caps`) to convey airy boutique sophistication.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column layout structured around ample breathing room:

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (< 640px, 4 columns, 16px margins), Tablet (641px–1024px, 8 columns, 24px margins), Desktop (> 1025px, 12 columns, max-width 1360px centered, 32px margins).
- **Product Grids**: Desktop maintains a spacious 3 or 4-column cadence. Mobile uses a clean 2-column masonry or card rhythm with 12px horizontal gutters to highlight fabric textures and drape photography.
- **Vertical Flow**: Sections are separated with generous vertical spacing (4rem to 6rem on desktop) to evoke the pacing of an upscale lookbook.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through ambient rose-infused drop shadows and soft tonal layering rather than harsh cutoffs:

- **Atmospheric Shadows**: Shadows carry a warm chocolate and blush tint: `0 8px 30px rgba(74, 46, 43, 0.06)`. This replaces grey shadows with a soft glow that feels warm and illuminated.
- **Floating Cart & Modals**: Overlay components utilize a dual-layer treatment: a faint 1px border (`#E8D7D3`) paired with a deeper, diffuse shadow (`0 20px 48px rgba(74, 46, 43, 0.12)`).
- **Surface Layering**: Cards sit seamlessly on the `#FDF6F3` background with solid white `#FFFFFF` fills, relying on hairline borders (`#F0E2DE`) and light hover lift for micro-feedback.

## Shapes
A balanced rounded silhouette (`roundedness: 2`) echoes the fluid arches, drape curves, and circular emblem of the brand:

- Base UI elements (inputs, standard buttons, notification toasts) use 8px (`0.5rem`).
- Product cards, interactive image showcases, and promo banners use 16px (`1rem`) to keep the visual tone organic and gentle.
- Swatches, badges, and quick-filter chips use fully pill-shaped radii (9999px) for friendly tactile touch targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#4A2E2B`, label `#FDF6F3`, 8px radius, height 48px. On hover, shifts to an opulent deep cocoa glow with a subtle upward translate (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary / Wishlist**: Background transparent, border 1.5px solid `#DDA5A5`, text `#4A2E2B`. On hover, fills with delicate `#F7EBEA`.
- **Soft Accent CTA**: Background `#DDA5A5`, text `#4A2E2B`, semi-bold, ideal for cart promotions and highlight banners.

### Product Cards
- Contained within white canvas surfaces with soft 16px corner curves.
- Aspect ratio 3:4 for imagery to showcase full hijab drapes and styling details.
- Floating quick-add button appearing on hover/focus as a pill button in translucent white glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`).
- Color swatches shown as circular 16px dots with a 2px active ring outline in `#4A2E2B`.

### Form Fields & Search
- Inputs have a solid white base, 8px radius, and border in `#E8D7D3`.
- Active focus state features an outline in `#DDA5A5` with a soft rose ambient ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(221, 165, 165, 0.25)`).
- Floating label or placeholder text rendered in `#8C706B`.

### Badges & Filter Chips
- Pill-shaped tags with 12px height padding.
- Bestseller & New: Delicate background `#F6E3E3` with deep text `#4A2E2B` and subtle 1px border `#DDA5A5`.
- Selected filter state: `#4A2E2B` background with white text and smooth spring transition.

### Fabric Swatch Selector & Drawer
- Slide-over mini-cart drawer finished in alabaster neutral with soft divider rules in `#F0E2DE`.
- Includes fabric indicators (e.g., "Mousseline de Soie", "Jersey Premium", "Soie de Médine") styled in `label-caps` for clear product authenticity.
